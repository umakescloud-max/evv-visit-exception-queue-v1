Every Monday, someone on a home care billing team pastes three exports into a spreadsheet and hunts for missing clock-outs by eye.

I built a concept prototype that puts those visits in one queue. Each flagged visit shows a plain reason, like "No clock-out recorded", and how many business days it has waited.

In the demo, one visit looks wrong only because the client moved and the address was never updated. The coordinator fixes the address, attaches the family's confirmation, and approves it herself. Nothing goes to claims or payroll without her click.
